@User 033 That looks great! There's just one small, subtle formatting issue. Mathematical functions (like the sine) are typically indicated with a \. So the correct format for you function would be

$$
f(x) = \sin x
$$

rather than just

$$
f(x) = sin x
$$

That sort of thing should usually be caught with the proofreader you can find under sparkly AI menu.
