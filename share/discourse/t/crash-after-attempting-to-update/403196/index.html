Crash after attempting to update

TroLLoBlogger

After trying to update Discourse through the admin panel, the process crashed, and my forum wouldn’t load. Fortunately, there is a solution to the problem

Open a terminal on your server, then run the following commands:

cd /var/discourse

./launcher rebuild app

After the rebuild was completed, everything went back to normal for me and the forum was updated. I’m also leaving a log of the error that caused the problem because it is related to a Discourse plugin.
