Yes, this usually means Bing has found your URL but is unable to crawl it properly. In most cases, the issue is caused by a blocked robots.txt, firewall or Cloudflare restrictions, server errors, or very thin content. Check that your site returns a 200 OK status, submit your sitemap, and request indexing again after fixing any crawl issues.
