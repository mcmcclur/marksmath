High context topic cards are now enabled by default in the Horizon theme

martin

A few months ago we introduced Horizon: High Context Topic Cards for the Horizon theme. Now, using an upcoming change, we are making this the new default topic card style for Horizon.

As a quick recap, here is what high context topic cards expose in the topic list:

Horizon: High Context Topic Cards

These cards surface key signals at a glance, pulling in data from familiar favourites:

  • Solved (1)
  • Topic Voting (2)
  • Assign (3)
  • Good old tags (4)
  • Excerpts

We have also rearranged the layout, refined spacing and typography, and expanded the last-reply preview; aiming for a balanced experience.

Upcoming change

You can enable this upcoming change early at /admin/config/upcoming-changes:

image974×151 53.9 KB

This upcoming change will only show if Horizon is the default theme for your site, or if it’s user-selectable. Brand new Discourse sites will also automatically get high context topic cards enabled, they will not see this upcoming change either.

When the upcoming change reaches Stable, we will remove it. Most sites will have transitioned to high context topic cards by then, but admins will still be able to switch back to simple topic cards with the “topic card high context” theme setting:
