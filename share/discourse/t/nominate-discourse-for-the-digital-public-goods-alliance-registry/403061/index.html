Nominate Discourse for the Digital Public Goods Alliance registry

tobiaseigen

Discourse is one of the only open source products in my tech stack that is not in the Digital Public Goods registry, which connects open source software with the UN sustainable development goals. I think nomination is pretty easy and I’m happy to do it, but I think it would make sense for the Discourse team to do it because it’s a good marketing opportunity. Starts at the link below:

Digital Public Goods Alliance - Promoting digital public goods to create a more...

Digital Public Goods Alliance - Digital Public Goods Alliance

Unlocking the potential of open-source technologies for a more equitable world. Explore and learn more about digital public goods The Digital Public Goods Alliance is a multi-stakeholder initiative that accelerates the attainment of the Sustainable...

The registry itself is a super interesting list of projects to scroll through - seems there’s open source for every function you can imagine! It has a github activity indicator for each project, so you can see which ones are active (or completely dead).

Each project also gets a very nice page in the registry explaining how they meet the criteria for inclusion,for example ghost.

Here’s how Nextcloud and Ghost talk about the DPG on their blogs:

Nextcloud – 5 Feb 26

Nextcloud's long-standing commitment to open source, privacy, and data control has been recognized by the Digital Public Good Alliance.
