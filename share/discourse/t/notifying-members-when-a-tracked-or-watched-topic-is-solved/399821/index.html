Notifying members when a Tracked or Watched topic is solved

lindsey

When a member of your community is Tracking or Watching a topic in a support category (using the Discourse Solved plugin), they will receive a notification when that topic is marked as solved. This makes it easier for members to get answers to questions without having to check in on the topic manually or create a duplicate question topic.

The member who marks the solution will not receive this notification.

I’ve gotten a lot of negative feedback about this recently from people who want to see when they, themselves get a best answer, but not when someone else does, so they can’t just turn it off.

Also, I have reports that people who are watching the first post only are getting notifications even though that isn’t a first post, and also even if they just read the post and are not watching it.

Some of them may be overlooking a certain setting… not saying all of the self-reporting is necessarily accurate… but it does seem like there should be a way to go back to the old way where they only got notified for their own best answers.

Not everything you read is in pursuit of an answer, especially on our site. They are looking to see if they can help, and so unless it is about their own answer, it is arguably unproductive/unhelpful to get those notifications.
